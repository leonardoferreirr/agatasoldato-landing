Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Agata Posh respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide through our website, appointment requests, and communications.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, appointment preferences, and details submitted when requesting information about our services or training programs.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and manage appointments, provide customer support, and communicate about our services. With your consent, we may also send promotional messages and special offers.

Providing a phone number alone does not enroll you in promotional text messages.

Mobile Information and SMS Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or disclose your mobile information to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional activities.

Your SMS enrollment records and consent remain confidential and are not provided to other organizations for their own use or marketing.

Text Messaging

If you opt in, you may receive text messages from Agata Posh about appointments, customer support, service updates, or promotional offers, depending on the consent you provide.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from text messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or call 512-502-5022.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing services.

Information Protection and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

We retain information as necessary to provide services, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time.

External Links

Our website may link to third-party websites or booking platforms. Their privacy policies govern information you provide directly to them.
