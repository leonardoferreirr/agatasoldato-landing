Payment Plans & Financing

Beauty Now. Flexible Payments.

Agata Medical Aesthetics offers payment plan options to help make services and multi-treatment plans more accessible. Clients can ask about available financing during their consultation and review eligibility, terms, and payment options before scheduling.

Financing can be especially helpful for clients combining multiple services or choosing a higher-value treatment plan.

How It Works

  1. Choose your service or personalized treatment plan.
  2. Ask our team about currently available payment plan options.
  3. Complete the financing provider's application and review the terms offered directly by that provider.
  4. Once approved and all studio requirements are completed, schedule your service according to the applicable booking and financing policies.

Important Financing Policy

Financed services may be subject to separate cancellation, refund, rescheduling, and provider terms. Clients should review and agree to the applicable financing agreement and Agata Medical Aesthetics policies before completing a financed purchase. Financing approval and terms are determined by the financing provider.
