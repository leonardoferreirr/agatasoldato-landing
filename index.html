Four steps, and none of them rushed.

Most of the work happens before the machine is ever switched on. By the time we start, you have already approved the shape in the mirror.

01

Consultation

Skin type, medical history, the shape you have and the one you want. We also test colour against your undertone.

02

Mapping

The design is drawn on your face and measured against your bone structure. Nothing begins until you say yes to the mirror.

03

The session

Numbing first, then pigment in slow passes. Two to three hours, and you go home with written aftercare.

04

Perfecting

Six to eight weeks later we look at how your skin healed and adjust colour and density. That visit is part of the price.

Before you book.

One to three years, depending on your skin, your sun exposure and the technique used. Oily skin fades faster and needs a refresh sooner. It fades softly, so it never leaves you with a hard line to explain.

Less than most people expect. The area is numbed before we start and topped up during the session. Most clients describe it as light scratching, and plenty fall asleep in the chair.

Two. The first session builds the shape and the base colour, and the perfecting visit six to eight weeks later adjusts what your skin kept. Both are included in the price.

Often yes. Send a clear photo in daylight, with no makeup, before you book. Some cases need lightening or removal first, and I will tell you that upfront instead of pigmenting over a problem.
